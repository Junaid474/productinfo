Blazing Fast DDR5 Speeds

Step into the new era of computing with DDR5 technology. The Predator Vesta II runs at a massive 6000MHz (PC5-48000), offering double the bandwidth of previous-generation DDR4 memory. This translates to incredibly smooth gameplay, faster rendering times, and seamless multitasking.

Stunning RGB Aesthetics

Customize your rig with dynamic, frosted RGB lighting. The Vesta II is fully compatible with major motherboard RGB sync software, allowing you to seamlessly integrate its lighting effects with the rest of your system components.

Built for Stability

Featuring on-die ECC (Error-Correcting Code) and a robust metallic heat sink, this RAM is designed to maintain extreme stability even under heavy loads and high overclocks. It fully supports both Intel XMP 3.0 and AMD EXPO profiles for easy one-click setup.
