Deep Clean with 46,000Hz Waves

This compact ultrasonic cleaner is a game-changer for maintaining your valuables. By generating 46,000Hz of high-frequency ultrasonic waves, it creates millions of microscopic cleansing bubbles. This process, known as cavitation, gently but powerfully lifts dirt, grime, and oils from the intricate crevices of your items without the need for harsh chemicals or abrasive scrubbing.

Premium 304 Stainless Steel Tank

Durability meets hygiene. The interior boasts a generous 500ml tank constructed from food-grade 304 stainless steel, ensuring it resists rust and corrosion over time. The exterior is wrapped in a sleek, durable ABS shell that looks great on any bathroom counter or vanity.

Versatile Everyday Use

While perfect for restoring the brilliant shine to diamond rings, gold necklaces, and platinum bands, its uses go far beyond jewelry. It is highly effective for cleaning eyeglasses, sunglasses, waterproof watches, makeup brushes, dentures, pacifiers, and even intricate metal pen nibs.
