Ultimate Gaming Performance

The AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D takes gaming performance to the next level with its revolutionary 3D V-Cache technology. By stacking additional L3 cache directly on the processor, it drastically reduces latency and boosts frame rates in cache-sensitive games.

Zen 5 Architecture

Built on the latest Zen 5 architecture, the 9800X3D offers significant IPC improvements and incredible power efficiency. It fits seamlessly into the AM5 socket, ensuring longevity and upgradeability with support for DDR5 and PCIe 5.0.
