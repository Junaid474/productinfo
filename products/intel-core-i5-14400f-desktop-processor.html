Solid Mid-Range Gaming

The Intel Core i5-14400F is a powerhouse for budget and mid-range gaming builds. By combining 6 Performance-cores and 4 Efficient-cores, it handles modern gaming workloads efficiently while leaving plenty of headroom for background tasks.

LGA 1700 Socket Compatibility

As part of the 14th generation Raptor Lake Refresh lineup, the 14400F drops right into existing LGA 1700 motherboards (600 and 700 series), allowing for easy upgrades without needing a brand new motherboard.

Important Note on Graphics

Please note that the 'F' suffix in the processor name indicates that this CPU does not feature integrated graphics. You will absolutely need a dedicated graphics card (GPU) to use this processor.
